## stone-lit.png の生成プロンプト

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: luxury Japanese satirical shop hero and product photograph, square composition. Primary request: an utterly ordinary small gray roadside pebble photographed with the meticulous lighting of an exclusive fine jewelry boutique. One smooth irregular dull gray stone, natural tiny imperfections and fine mineral texture, emphatically NOT a gemstone, no crystals or facets. Resting physically on a low charcoal velvet display plinth inside a dark navy-black studio. Beautiful controlled narrow warm-white spotlights, soft cool rim light, delicate shadows and realistic contact shadow, extremely refined museum product photography, quiet premium atmosphere, ample dark negative space around stone. Stone centered, about one third of image width, camera three-quarter view, macro-lens detail, realistic material. Palette charcoal, dark navy, subdued warm gold highlights. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no sparkles, no glow, no floating stone.
